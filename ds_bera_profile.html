About

Caring for newborns, patients and families.

Dr. Susanta Bera is a general physician who earned his MBBS from the historic R.G. Kar Medical College & Hospital, Kolkata, and holds the CBET qualification. Registered with the West Bengal Medical Council (Reg. No. 63190), he brings close to two decades of hands-on clinical experience across general medicine and radiology.

He serves as Medical Officer in the Sick Newborn Care Unit at Jhargram Government Medical College & Hospital. Alongside, he practises at Staar Diagnostic Imaging Clinic, Midnapore, where he performs ultrasonography (USG) and sees patients as a general physician — so the doctor who examines you can also review your scan with you.

Radiology · Ultrasonography

What is a USG scan?

Ultrasonography (USG) uses high-frequency sound waves — not X-rays — to create live pictures of organs inside the body. A small probe with a little gel is moved over the skin, and the returning echoes are turned into images on the screen in real time.

No radiationSafe for children and adults, and routinely used in pregnancy.
Painless & quickMost scans take about 10–20 minutes.
Real-timeShows organs, movement and blood flow as it happens.
Scan + consultationFindings are reviewed with you by the same doctor.

Commonly used for

Whole abdomenLiver & gall bladderKidney, ureter & bladder (KUB)PelvisPregnancy (obstetric)Thyroid & neckSoft-tissue swellings

Before your scan: for an abdominal scan you may be asked to fast for a few hours; for a pelvic or KUB scan, drink water so your bladder is full. The clinic will tell you exactly what to do.

Determination of the sex of the foetus is not done — it is prohibited by law under the PC&PNDT Act.
